My story

hi, i'm Josée.

I started as a rural mail carrier. Then I tried the get-rich-quick schemes, and I earned nothing from every single one.

I have not had a job in over 17 years. I have worked for myself, full time, ever since.

So I built my own digital products and affiliate offers, and that finally worked. Over 30,000 products sold, a top 3% affiliate, a Facebook community of 140K. Then I opened a digital marketing agency and ran it for seven years on 80-hour weeks.

Then my body said stop. I burned out, collapsed and ended up in the ICU. I shut the agency down.

I rebuilt quietly, with no face on camera and no staff. Instead, I built thirteen AI helpers that do the work, so the business no longer depends on me for every task. It is the best work life I have had.

Now I show people how to build the same thing, one simple step at a time.
