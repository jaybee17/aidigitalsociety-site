Questions

quick answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. The challenge is free. You only need a Claude account.

Do I need the paid Claude plan?

No. You can start on the free plan. Free Claude can run out of messages for the day. If that happens, come back later and carry on. Prices change, so check claude.com/pricing.

Do I have to show my face?

No. Not on any day.

When does it start?

A new round starts every Monday. Sign up any day and you begin with the next group.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing happens. Pick it up the next day. Nobody is timing you.

I have never used Claude. Is that a problem?

No. Day 1 starts from zero.

Will I make money in seven days?

I do not promise that. This challenge teaches the first steps. It is not a promise of income.
